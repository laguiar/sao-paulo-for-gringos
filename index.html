Art & Culture

Ipiranga Museum

Historic museum showcasing Brazilian independence and São Paulo's heritage. One of the most important museums in Brazil, featuring colonial architecture and Brazilian history exhibits.

Visit Website

MASP - São Paulo Museum of Art

Iconic museum with an impressive collection of Western art in Latin America. Famous for its unique architecture and world-class exhibitions featuring Brazilian and international artists.

Visit Website

The Football Museum

Celebrate Brazil's football culture and history in this interactive museum. Located in Pacaembu Stadium, it showcases Brazil's rich football heritage and legendary players.

Visit Website

Sightseeing

Ibirapuera Park

São Paulo's most famous park, perfect for leisure and outdoor activities. Known as the "Central Park of São Paulo" with beautiful gardens, museums, and cultural venues.

Visit Website

Liberdade

Explore the vibrant Japanese neighborhood with authentic culture and cuisine. The largest Japanese community outside Japan, featuring traditional markets, restaurants, and cultural festivals.

TripAdvisor

Paulista Avenue

Iconic avenue, especially beautiful on Sunday afternoons when closed to traffic. São Paulo's most famous street featuring cultural centers, shopping, and the city's financial district.

TripAdvisor

São Paulo Cathedral & City Center

Historic church and downtown area - recommended with a guided tour or local companion.

Pastel de Feira & Caldo de Cana

Must try! Pastel de Feira is the most traditional Brazilian street food in São Paulo and many other areas in Brazil, it can be found everywhere on street markets and small shops. It's traditionally paired with Caldo de Cana (Sugar Cane juice), a legendary Brazilian street food combo.
