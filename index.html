Pastel de Feira & Caldo de Cana

Must try!

Pastel de Feira is the most traditional Brazilian street food in São Paulo and many other areas in Brazil, it can be found everywhere on street markets and small shops. It's traditionally paired with Caldo de Cana (Sugar Cane juice), a legendary Brazilian street food combo.
